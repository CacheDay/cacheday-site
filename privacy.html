CacheDay Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date] · Version: [1.0]

CacheDay collects only what it needs to plan your bills around your paychecks. We never sell your personal information and never see your bank password. This policy covers the CacheDay app, cacheday.com, and the beta waitlist, and works with our Terms of Service at cacheday.com/terms.

1. Information we collect

Type What it includes Where it comes from
Account Name, email, sign-in method (Apple, Google, or email with a one-time code), plan (Free or Connect) You
Bills and plan Bill names, categories, budgets, due dates, payment method, payment links and apps you add, amounts you log as paid, automatic transfers You
Pay schedule How often you're paid, paydays, and base pay estimate You
Bank data (Connect only) Account names, last four digits of account numbers, balances, transactions, and paycheck deposits Your bank, through Plaid
Settings Theme, notification choices, Face ID lock setting (we never receive your Face ID data) You
Device and usage Device type, app version, crash reports, push notification token, and how features are used Your device
Support and feedback Messages, issue reports, and suggestions you send us You
Waitlist (website) Name, email, phone type, how you track bills now, and any note you add when joining the beta waitlist You

What we don't collect: your bank username or password, your full account numbers, your Social Security number, or your card details for Connect (Apple or Google handles payment).

2. How we use your information

  • To run CacheDay: plan bills across your paychecks, estimate what's left, track extra cash, and build extra check plans.

  • With Connect: match bank transactions to bills to mark them paid and cleared, detect paydays and deposit amounts, and suggest bills.

  • To send the notifications you choose, such as due-date reminders and payday alerts.

  • To run the beta and waitlist: invite testers, email you about the launch, and send any offer you're eligible for, such as a founding-member code.

  • To provide support, keep accounts secure, prevent fraud, and fix bugs.

  • To improve CacheDay using aggregated or de-identified usage information. Our internal reports show totals, such as sign-ups per day, not information about individual people.

  • To meet legal obligations.

3. How we share it

We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help run CacheDay under contracts that limit their use of your data: hosting and database [e.g., Supabase], bank connections (Plaid), subscription management [e.g., RevenueCat], crash reporting [e.g., Sentry], email [email provider], website hosting [website host], and push notifications (Apple and Google).

  • Apple or Google, to process Connect subscriptions and promotional codes.

  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect users, the public, or CacheDay.

  • A business transfer, such as a merger or sale, where the new owner keeps this policy's protections.

  • Anyone else, only with your permission.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

  • We don't show ads in CacheDay or let advertisers use your data.

  • We don't make payments or move money from your accounts.

5. Bank connections and Plaid

When you connect a bank with Connect, Plaid collects data from your bank and shares it with us. Plaid handles your bank login; CacheDay receives only a secure access token and the read-only data listed above. Plaid's use of your data is covered by Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect a bank anytime in Settings. If you cancel Connect or delete your account, we disconnect your banks for you. We then revoke access with Plaid and delete that account's stored data within [30] days.

6. How we protect your information

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; bank access tokens are stored with additional encryption.

  • Access to production systems is limited to people who need it and protected with multi-factor authentication.

  • We monitor for unusual activity, test our security regularly, and keep a written security program and incident response plan.

  • You can turn on Face ID lock in the app for extra protection on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you and regulators as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, bills, and plan: while your account is open.

  • Bank transactions: up to [24] months for Connect features, or until you disconnect the bank.

  • Waitlist information: until launch plus [12] months, or until you ask us to remove it or unsubscribe.

  • Deleted accounts: removed within [30] days, except backups, which roll off within [90] days, and records we must keep by law.

  • Aggregated or de-identified data that can't identify you may be kept longer.

8. Your choices and rights

  • In the app: edit or delete any bill, disconnect banks, turn notifications on or off, and delete your account in Settings.

  • Emails: every launch or waitlist email has an unsubscribe link.

  • By request: email [privacy email] to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. We'll respond within 45 days and may need to verify your identity.

  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia, or Connecticut), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and limit use of your information. We won't treat you differently for using them.

9. Children

CacheDay is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has signed up, contact us and we'll delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows the current version, and we keep copies of earlier versions.
